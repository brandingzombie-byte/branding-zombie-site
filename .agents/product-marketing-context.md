## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A local design + print shop in Cumming, GA that gives small businesses a real brand — logos, websites, signs, and shirts — at flat prices, in days not months.

**What it does:** Branding Zombie Designs is a solo-led studio handling brand identity, web design, print/signage, and small-business marketing for trades, restaurants, retail, and first-time entrepreneurs across Forsyth County and North Metro Atlanta. Output ranges from a single logo to a full launch kit (logo + site + cards + flyers).

**Product category:** Local small-business design studio. Sits on the same "shelf" as sign shops, freelance designers, and regional agencies — but priced and scoped between Fiverr and Atlanta agencies.

**Product type:** Service business — productized packages + custom quotes. Some products (apparel, print) are reseller pass-through with a flat decoration markup; design/dev is in-house.

**Business model:**
- Productized tiers: $997 Startup Special, $2,800 / $4,500 / $8,000+ packages
- A la carte services: Logo from $750, Web from $1,500, Print from $75, AI Workflows from $750
- Decoration markup: 1.25× wholesale (per active project memory)
- Localized: GA sales tax (~7% Forsyth), local pickup/delivery available

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** "My business looks amateur online and on the truck, and every quote I get is either $50 from Fiverr (junk) or $20,000 from an Atlanta agency (impossible)."

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **Fiverr / 99designs / AI generators:** Output is generic, no production knowledge, no follow-through, end up worse than starting
- **$69/month subscription agencies:** Template flips with no strategy, lock you into their CMS, generic results
- **Atlanta agencies:** $15K+ minimums, 4-month timelines, treat SMBs like an afterthought
- **DIY (Wix, Canva):** Owner can't get it to look real, weeks of nights/weekends, still amateur

**What it costs them:**
- Lost local calls because the site loads slow or looks fake
- Embarrassment showing up to job sites in worn shirts and an ugly wrap
- Missed online orders / form fills / book-now flows
- Mental load of "I should fix this" sitting in the back of every workday

**Emotional tension:**
- "I don't want to feel dumb being sold something I don't understand"
- "What if they're going to surprise me with a bill later?"
- "Will they actually take me seriously as a small shop?"
- "I'm not artistic — I just need someone to tell me what to do"
