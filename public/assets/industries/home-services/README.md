# Home Services — page images

**Live page:** /industries/home-services
(lawn care, cleaning, pest, pool, pressure washing, junk removal, handyman)

**How this works (same as Tier 1):** Drop your real home-service brand reference
assets into this folder — logos, truck/trailer lettering art, door hangers, yard
sign art, crew apparel. Then tell Claude *"generate the home services images"* and
it composites your artwork into the lifestyle shots below, SEO-names them,
optimizes to WebP, and wires them into `industries.ts`.

Until then the page shows on-brand placeholders describing each shot.
