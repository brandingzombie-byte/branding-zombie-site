# Ecommerce & DTC Brands — page images

**Live page:** /industries/ecommerce-dtc
(Shopify, Amazon, DTC, subscription)

**How this works (same as Tier 1):** Drop your real store/brand reference assets
into this folder — logos, store screenshots, product renders, packaging art, ad
creative. Then tell Claude *"generate the ecommerce images"* and it composites your
artwork into the lifestyle shots below, SEO-names them, optimizes to WebP, and
wires them into `industries.ts`.

Until then the page shows on-brand placeholders describing each shot.

| Slot | Aspect | What we'll generate |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | square | A laptop + phone on a dark desk showing the same DTC store, a finger over a glowing add-to-cart button. |
| Storefront, two screens | wide | A clean Shopify homepage on a laptop with the same store on a leaning phone, brand color across both. |
| Product page that converts | tall | A tall phone screenshot of a product page: hero photo, star reviews, FAQ block, sticky add-to-cart bar. |
| Email plus paid ad | square | An abandoned-cart email next to a matching Meta ad for the same product — one consistent brand. |
| Branded unboxing | square | Hands opening a branded mailer box on a porch, tissue + thank-you card with the store URL. |

Reference-asset tip: drop the store logo + a couple of product renders (or a live
store screenshot) — those drive the storefront, product-page, and unboxing shots.
