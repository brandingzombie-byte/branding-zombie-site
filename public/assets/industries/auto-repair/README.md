# Auto & Repair — page images

**Live page:** /industries/auto-repair

**How this works (same as Tier 1):** Drop your real auto-shop brand reference
assets into this folder — logos, sign art, shirt/embroidery art, business cards,
banners. Then tell Claude *"generate the auto images"* and it composites your
artwork into the lifestyle shots below, SEO-names them, optimizes to WebP, and
wires them into `industries.ts`.

Until then the page shows on-brand placeholders describing each shot.

| Slot | Aspect | What we'll generate |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | square | Independent shop front at golden hour, bay doors open, a clean backlit sign over the lot, a tech by a lifted truck. |
| Shop sign & building front | wide | A new channel-letter sign spanning the shop facade, bay numbers above each door. |
| Branded mechanic shirts | square | Navy work shirts with an embroidered shop logo + name patch on a rack. |
| Window & banner graphics | wide | Storefront glass with crisp vinyl lettering (brakes, alignment, tires) and an hours decal. |
| Cards & service price board | tall | Front-counter flat-lay: business cards beside a framed printed service/price menu. |

Reference-asset tip: drop the shop logo (clean PNG/SVG/large JPG) first — it
drives every slot. Add sign/shirt/card art if you have it.
