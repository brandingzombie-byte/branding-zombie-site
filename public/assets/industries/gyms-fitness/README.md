# Gyms & Fitness — page images

**Live page:** /industries/gyms-fitness

**How this works (same as Tier 1):** Drop your real gym/fitness brand reference
assets into this folder — logos, member apparel art, wall-graphic art, schedule
designs, anything. Then tell Claude *"generate the gyms images"* and it composites
your artwork into the lifestyle shots below with Higgsfield / Nano Banana, names
them for SEO, optimizes to WebP, and wires them into `industries.ts`.

Until then the page shows on-brand placeholders describing each shot.

| Slot | Aspect | What we'll generate |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | square | A turf-and-rig gym at golden hour with the logo painted across the back wall, members training in the foreground. |
| Wall logo | wide | Brick gym wall with the logo painted ~6ft tall above a row of loaded squat racks. |
| Member hoodies | square | Flat-lay of folded branded hoodies and tees in the gym's colors. |
| Class schedule board | tall | Printed weekly class schedule + membership board mounted by the check-in desk. |
| Workout post | square | High-energy Instagram-style frame of a member mid-lift with the logo + class time in the corner. |

Reference-asset tip: a clean logo (PNG/SVG/large JPG) is the most important drop —
it drives every slot. Add apparel/signage art if you have it.
