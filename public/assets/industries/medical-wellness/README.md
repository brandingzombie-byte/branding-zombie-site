# Medical, Dental & Wellness — page images

**Live page:** /industries/medical-wellness
(dental, med spa, chiro, therapy, optometry, clinics)

**How this works (same as Tier 1):** Drop your real practice brand reference
assets into this folder — logos, signage art, intake/folder art, appointment
cards, website screenshots. Then tell Claude *"generate the medical images"* and
it composites your artwork into the lifestyle shots below, SEO-names them,
optimizes to WebP, and wires them into `industries.ts`.

Until then the page shows on-brand placeholders describing each shot.

| Slot | Aspect | What we'll generate |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | square | A calm clinic reception: a backlit logo sign on a clean accent wall, chairs, a plant, a tablet check-in, soft daylight. |
| Reception sign | wide | A dimensional logo sign above the front desk with soft uplighting, waiting chairs blurred in front. |
| Booking site | square | A laptop + phone showing the practice site with a clear Book Appointment button and time-slot picker. |
| Intake folder | tall | A branded intake folder fanned open with new-patient forms + matching appointment cards. |
| Wellness post | square | A soft, calm Instagram post: muted palette, one line of advice, the logo small in the corner. |

Reference-asset tip: a clean practice logo is the key drop. Add interior/signage
photos or color references for a more on-brand result.
