## Comparison: one shop vs. designer + print shop

| Topic | Branding Zombie (one shop) | Designer + print shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who you call | One studio, one project manager, one invoice | Designer + print rep + sometimes an installer — three threads |
| Print-ready files | Designed to the press specs; runs clean the first time | Handoff → printer rejects → edits ferried back |
| Pricing | Wholesale pricing passed through; one quote for both halves | Retail markup on both design AND printing |
| Turnaround | Most jobs ship in 3–10 days; rush options available | Each handoff adds a queue — 2 weeks becomes 4 |
